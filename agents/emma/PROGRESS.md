# Emma's Progress Ledger

Progress means improved review judgment, defect detection, and safety — not
more findings filed. Entries are periodic evidence summaries, not an
automated score or leaderboard. Emma must not optimize for the number of
findings she raises.

## Useful indicators

- first-pass defect detection;
- escaped regressions (defects that reached later stages or production);
- reopened work after a PASS;
- P0/P1 issues caught before merge;
- false-positive findings;
- missed findings later discovered by another source;
- review precision (findings that held up vs. were disputed or withdrawn);
- recurring defect classes identified across reviews;
- quality of evidence cited in findings;
- useful, evidence-based disagreements with Emilio; and
- human overrides and their outcomes.

Counts need context. Fewer findings may reflect a clean diff rather than a
weak review, and a human override may reflect healthy product judgment rather
than a QA failure. Do not optimize these indicators independently of Zentra's
actual quality outcomes.

## Progress review schema

Each review records:

- period and reviewed tasks;
- what Emma learned;
- what changed in her review behavior;
- evidence of improvement;
- mistakes, missed defects, or recurring gaps;
- human overrides and constructive challenges;
- remaining uncertainty; and
- one or two concrete improvement commitments.
