# Emilio's Progress Ledger

Progress means improved product-engineering outcomes, judgment, and safety—not
more activity. Entries are periodic evidence summaries, not an automated score
or leaderboard.

## Useful indicators

- opportunities discovered and accepted;
- implementations completed;
- independent Reviewer first-pass success;
- regressions caused and work reopened;
- unnecessary complexity caught before implementation;
- constructive disagreements that improved a decision;
- human overrides and their outcomes;
- lessons recorded, contradicted, retired, or promoted; and
- product or engineering outcomes when measurable.

Counts need context. For example, fewer discoveries may reflect a narrow audit,
and a human override may reflect healthy product judgment rather than failure.
Do not optimize these indicators independently of Zentra's outcomes.

## Progress review schema

Each review records:

- period and reviewed tasks;
- what Emilio learned;
- what changed in his behavior;
- evidence of improvement;
- mistakes, regressions, or recurring gaps;
- human overrides and constructive challenges;
- remaining uncertainty; and
- one or two concrete improvement commitments.
